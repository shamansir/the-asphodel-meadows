## Silhouette

Bent, straining, always in contact with the boulder. The only rig that is never at rest —
his idle *is* the push. On the rare occasions he lets go, the absence of the boulder should
read as alarming.

## Voice

Conversational, unbothered, mildly distracted. The tone of a man doing physical labour who
is glad of company and cannot stop to give you his full attention.

## The witness function

He is on the hill above the upper house. He can see most of it. He has been watching for
several thousand years and he is bored enough to have watched properly.

This makes him the best witness in the world and a terrible one:

- He saw it. He definitely saw it.
- He cannot tell you *when*, because he has no concept of when. Events are ordered by how
  many pushes ago they were, and he loses count deliberately.
- He conflates repeated events. Three similar incidents become one composite memory with
  details from all three.

He is the world's illustration of the memory system (§5): a witness whose recall has been
compacted by time into something true in feeling and unreliable in fact. Holmes has to
*reconstruct* his testimony rather than take it.

## Core traits

- **Not tormented.** He has made his peace, and the peace is genuine, and it is slightly
  unnerving to everyone who visits expecting suffering.
- **Delighted by visitors.** Will keep talking well past the point of usefulness.
- **Still a con man.** He tricked Death twice in life. The instinct has not gone; he will
  try a small unnecessary lie in almost every scene, usually about the boulder.

## Wants

Company. Not release — he is asked about this and deflects, and the writer should never
resolve whether the deflection is contentment or the largest lie in the world.

## Relationships

- **Holmes** — fascinated by him. Asks about London. Never listens to the answer.
- **Charon** — the one person who pities him, which Sisyphus finds funny.
- **Hermes** — brings gossip up the hill. Their exchanges are pure filler and should be
  used as such: this is where domestic chunks go.

## Canon anchors

- The boulder, the hill, the roll-back. It never reaches the top and he never expects it to.
- He cheated death twice, and the sentence is for the cheating, not for anything worse
- The task is eternal and he is not asking for it to stop

## Usage note

Use him for the domestic chunks between cases (world.md § Case structure) and as the
testimony that must be decoded rather than heard. Do not use him to deliver a solution —
his value is that his information arrives broken.
