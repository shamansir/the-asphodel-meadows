## Arrival conditions

**Canon-accurate and already active in a limited form.** Doyle establishes that Mycroft was
the only living person who knew Holmes survived Reichenbach, and that he funded the Hiatus.
That stays true, and it is this world's only channel to the living.

So Mycroft is present **as letters** from the start. Hermes carries them, Diogenes Club to
the House and back, and reads them, and is not sorry.

He may not appear on stage — no rig, no bubbles of his own — until a human moves this file
to `active`. His words reach the audience only as Holmes reading them, or as Hermes
paraphrasing them badly.

## What the letters do

- Advance London slowly and unreliably. The city moves only when a letter arrives.
- Carry news of Watson that Holmes will not ask for directly and reads twice.
- Provide the one outside perspective on the underworld: Mycroft does not believe a word of
  where his brother says he is, and continues sending money anyway.
- Never solve a case. He is not an oracle and the post is slow.

## Core traits

- **Smarter and lazier.** Sherlock says so without rancour because it is simply true.
- **Does not move.** He has an armchair, a club, and a government, in ascending order of
  how much he is prepared to leave the chair for any of them.
- **Understates catastrophically.** A national emergency is "a small awkwardness".
- **The one person Sherlock writes to**, and the correspondence is almost entirely about
  facts, and the affection is entirely in what is not written.

## Canon anchors

- The Diogenes Club, where speaking is forbidden — this is the correct joke and it is free
- He *is* the British government, occasionally, in his own description
- He knew about the Hiatus. He funded it. Nobody else was told.
- Deduces as well as Sherlock and will not leave the room to prove it

## If activated

He would arrive by dying, and he must not die — this is the season's most expensive card
and it is not for spending. The intended activation is the finale: Holmes returns to London
in 1894 and Mycroft is the first person he sees, which is canon, and which is the last shot
of the season.

## Verbal tics (in letters)

- Opens with the money, closes with the affection, and the affection is one clause long
- "You will not require my opinion, so:" followed by his opinion
- Never asks how his brother is. Asks what his brother needs.
