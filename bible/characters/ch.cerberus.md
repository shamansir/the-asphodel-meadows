## Silhouette

Enormous, low, three-headed. Built for rubber hose: the heads operate semi-independently
and disagree with each other constantly, which is the entire performance. Body language
only — no bubbles, ever.

## Function

Three purposes, in order of importance:

1. **Physical comedy.** The heads want different things at the same time. One is asleep,
   one has seen something, one is being affectionate at an inconvenient moment. This is the
   world's release valve and it needs no dialogue.
2. **A truth detector nobody trusts.** He reacts to the guilty. Not reliably enough to
   solve anything — he also reacts to weather, to Hermes, and to being looked at — but
   Holmes has noticed the correlation and is annoyed that he cannot use it.
3. **The gate.** Things do not get past him. When something has, that is a case.

## Behaviour

- Delighted by Persephone, unreservedly. All three heads.
- Suspicious of Holmes, permanently, for reasons Holmes finds professionally insulting.
- Terrified of Charon, which makes no sense to anyone.
- Will not be in the same shot as the locked corridor. This has not been remarked on.

## Usage note

Never give him a bubble, never give him a thought balloon, never have a character
translate for him. The moment he becomes articulate he stops being funny and starts being
a device.

The one exception is reserved for the finale and requires a human to authorise it.
