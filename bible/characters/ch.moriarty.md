## Arrival conditions

**The writer agent may not introduce this character.** A human moves this file to `active`.

He may not appear, speak, be named by any character, or be confirmed to exist until then.
What the agent *may* do — and should, steadily — is leave the traces listed below.

## The situation

He went over the falls too. He is therefore in this realm.

He is not in the filing system. Charon does not recall the passenger, and Charon does not
forget passengers. Minos has a gap in the Reichenbach ledger and has filed three queries
that were never answered. Hades knows something and has decided the disruption of
addressing it outweighs the risk of not.

Holmes does not know. The audience should work it out roughly a season ahead of him — that
gap is the pleasure.

## Traces the agent may plant

Background only, never as a case's solution, at most one per chunk:

- a form referenced by number that produces no record
- the locked corridor on the east side of the filing halls
- Cerberus refusing to be in a shot with it
- a name Charon declines to say, phrased as not recalling
- Minos changing the subject with unusual smoothness
- one of Sisyphus's composite memories containing a detail that belongs to nobody present

## Core traits, for when he arrives

- **The mirror, not the opposite.** Same faculty as Holmes, applied without the constraint
  of caring what is true.
- **Perfectly courteous.** He has never once raised his voice and does not intend to start.
- **Organisational, not violent.** He does not do things. Things happen, and afterwards it
  is clear they were arranged.
- **The only other person here who cannot cheat** — and has therefore been doing something
  far more interesting than the gods have noticed.

## Canon anchors

- Napoleon of crime; a web with him motionless at the centre
- Mathematician, author of a treatise on the binomial theorem — respectable, credentialed
- The oscillating head. Doyle gives him this and it is worth keeping.
- Reichenbach was mutual. Both men went over. Only one has been accounted for.

## Note on the finale

The season ends when Holmes learns what happened here and, as a consequence, returns to
London in 1894 (world.md § The season arc). This character is the mechanism of that ending.
Do not spend him early.
