## Arrival conditions

**The writer agent may not introduce this character.** A human moves this file to `active`.

He is alive, in London, and believes his closest friend died at Reichenbach. He is writing
the account of it, which in canon is *The Final Problem* — the story where Watson calls
Holmes "the best and the wisest man whom I have ever known" and means it as an obituary.

He cannot come here. That is the point of him this season.

## Permitted appearances before activation

Rarely, and each one should cost the chunk something:

- **In Mycroft's letters.** Secondhand, brief, factual. Holmes reads these twice and says
  nothing.
- **In memory.** 221B as Holmes recalls it, which is why it renders slightly wrong (see
  style.md § Palette) — a door on the wrong side, a street too short. Watson in memory is
  always facing away or mid-sentence. Never a clean look at him.
- **Scried, at a distance.** Seen and not reachable. Use once, at most, per season.

He does not speak in any of these. If he speaks, he is active, and that requires a human.

## Why he is held back

Hermes is Watson's replacement, he knows it, he is trying very hard, and he is not Watson.
That gap is the season's quiet ache and it only works while the original is absent.

Bringing Watson in early collapses the Hermes arc and removes the reason the ending means
anything.

## Core traits, for when he arrives

- **Competent, brave, and not a fool.** The bumbling Watson is a film invention. Doyle's
  is an army surgeon who has been shot and does not mention it.
- **The one who makes Holmes bearable**, socially and to the reader — he is the translation
  layer, and the story is colder without him.
- **A writer.** He shapes events into narrative, flatters his friend, and omits things. He
  is an unreliable chronicler too, in a completely different direction from Hermes.
- **Steady.** His defining quality is that he does not leave.

## Canon anchors

- Army surgeon, Afghanistan, invalided home, the wound moves and Doyle never fixed it
- He mourned. Three years. This happened and it is the emotional fact of the Hiatus.
- He faints when Holmes reveals himself in 1894 — the only time in canon he does

## Note on the finale

The last shot of the season is London, 1894. Whether Watson is in it is a human's decision.
